The Dreamed Adventure

12:30 Egzamin wstępny French documentary following the months-long, highly competitive admissions process at La Femis, one of the world's top film schools, where over 1,000 applicants compete for 40 spots.

The Competition

12:30–14:30 KNH 4 2h Francja, 2016 Q&A after

Dir. Claire Simon  ·  Instytucje / Struktury / Wspólnoty

Directed by Claire Simon (original title "Le Concours"), this observational documentary follows the multi-stage recruitment process at La Femis film school in Paris, where examiners narrow more than a thousand applicants down to just forty students. It alternates between candidates undergoing oral and written exams and professors debating what qualities and criteria should determine who gets in. Along the way it raises questions about how much a filmmaking career depends on an applicant's cultural and social background rather than raw talent. It won the Venezia Classici award at Venice in 2016 and the True Vision Award at True/False in 2017.
