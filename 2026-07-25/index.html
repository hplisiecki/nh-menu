12:15 Fukushima z Bélą Tarrem A three-hour documentary following legendary Hungarian director Béla Tarr as he runs a filmmaking workshop with students in Fukushima, Japan.

Fukushima with Béla Tarr

12:15–15:15 KNH 8 3h Japonia, 2024

Dir. Kaori Oda  ·  Pokazy Specjalne

Directed by Kaori Oda, one of Tarr's first students at his Film Factory school in Sarajevo, this documentary portrait observes Tarr teaching and mentoring young filmmakers on location in disaster-affected Fukushima. Oda's earlier films (Aragane, Toward a Common Tenderness, Cenote) premiered at Rotterdam and DOK Leipzig, and she won the 2020 Nagisa Oshima Award. Runtime is a full 180 minutes (3 hours).

Judgment in Hungary

12:30–14:18 KNH 4 1h 48m Węgry, Niemcy, Portugalia, 2013 Q&A after

Dir. Eszter Hajdú  ·  Instytucje / Struktury / Wspólnoty

Director Eszter Hajdú's crew filmed the entire courtroom process of a case in which six Roma people were killed and four suspects stood trial. 15:45 Pewnego razu w Harlemie Documentary revisiting a 1972 gathering of Harlem Renaissance-era writers, musicians and activists at Duke Ellington's home, filmed by William Greaves and completed decades later by his son.

Once Upon a Time in Harlem

15:45–17:25 KNH 7 1h 40m USA, 2026

Dir. William Greaves, David Greaves  ·  Międzynarodowy Konkurs Nowe Horyzonty

In 1972, filmmaker William Greaves filmed a one-off reunion of prominent Harlem Renaissance figures - writers, musicians, actors and activists - at the home of Duke Ellington. The footage sat unfinished until his son David Greaves, who operated a camera at the original event, completed the film a decade after his father's death. It documents hours of conversation and reflection among people who helped make Harlem a center of Black American culture. The film works as both a historical archive and a portrait of a vanished era.
