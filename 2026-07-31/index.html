15:45 Ogród Derek Jarman's 1990 experimental film-diary shot around his cottage garden on the English coast, made after his HIV diagnosis, with no conventional plot.

The Garden

15:45–17:29 DCF Lwów 1h 44m Wielka Brytania, Niemcy, Japonia, 1990

Dir. Derek Jarman  ·  Garden Party

A deeply personal, non-narrative work by British director Derek Jarman, made at Prospect Cottage on the Dungeness coast -- the garden he cultivated after being diagnosed with HIV. Rather than tell a story, the film assembles a kaleidoscope of surreal 16mm/35mm images -- wind-blown flowers, grass, waves and clotheslines -- interwoven with diary-like reflections later published as the book Modern Nature. It functions as a meditation on mortality and a critique of conservative Britain in the late 1980s/early 90s, with the garden as a symbol of paradise and refuge. Won an award at Berlinale 1991. Screens paired with the short "Piękne kwiaty i ich hodowla" (Beautiful Flowers and How to Grow Them) by Sarah Wood, as part of the "Garden Party" strand.
